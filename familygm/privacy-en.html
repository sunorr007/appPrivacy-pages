FamilyGM Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

In one sentence: FamilyGM is a game emulator that runs entirely on your device. Your game files, saves and settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves it is what ad delivery needs, plus anonymous usage analytics in the Google Play build only.

1. What we collect

  • Game files and saves: stored on your device only, never uploaded.
  • Library records: file name, file location and last-played time, kept in a local database.
  • Diagnostics: when the app crashes, the device model, Android version and stack trace are written to a local file. They are not sent anywhere automatically.
  • Settings: screen orientation, on-screen pad layout, cheat switches and purchase receipts, stored locally.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number or any account information. The app has no accounts.

2. Ads

Ads are served by three ad networks. To deliver and measure ads they may read your advertising ID (GAID / OAID), device model, OS version and network status:

  • GoogleGoogle AdMob — ad delivery and frequency capping
  • TencentGDT (Youlianghui) — ad delivery and frequency capping
  • ByteDancePangle — ad delivery and frequency capping

Personalized ads are on by default. No ad SDK requests ads before you agree to this policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings.

3. Analytics and crash reports

The build distributed through Google Play uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google LLC) to record events such as launch, download, play and purchase, plus crash logs, so we can improve the product. Builds distributed through other stores include no analytics service at all.

Analytics and crash collection also start only after you agree to this policy.

4. Purchases

Premium (subscription or one-time purchase) is billed by the app store you use. We never see your payment details and do not collect your name, email or account. Purchase receipts are stored on your own device to unlock ad removal there.

5. Permissions

  • Network: downloading games, cover images and ads.
  • Storage / all files access: read only when you tap Scan device, to find .nes and .zip game files. Other file types are not scanned.
  • Vibration: on-screen pad feedback.

6. Where game files come from

FamilyGM does not operate a game content server. The in-app curated list points to files published on third-party public sources, and you can hide that list at any time in Settings - Game resources. Once hidden, the app shows only the games you added yourself. We encourage you to use game files you own or are otherwise licensed to use.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all library records, saves, settings and purchase receipts on the device.
  • Your advertising ID is managed by Android and can be reset or deleted in system settings.
  • Android auto backup is enabled, so the system may back up app data to your own account. We cannot access that backup.

8. Children

FamilyGM is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe we have collected a child's information without knowledge, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page and in the app under About - Privacy policy, and the effective date above will be updated.
